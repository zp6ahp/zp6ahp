# Welcome to the ZP6AHP Radio Lab (DiyHam)! 📻🛠️

Hello, fellow hams and makers! My name is **Evgenii** (**UA6AHP**), and I’m a licensed amateur radio operator, maker, developer, and content creator. You might already know me from my **DiyHam** YouTube channel, where I share practical tutorials, 3D-printed gear, custom firmware, digital projects, and hands-on experiments for everyone who loves building things themselves.

### 📡 The Story Behind the Brand: ZP6AHP

Recently, I started a new chapter of my life and moved to **Paraguay**. While **UA6AHP** remains my active, official callsign, I decided to name my new international brand and project **ZP6AHP**. 

> **Important Note:** **ZP6AHP** is strictly a brand and project name right now, **not an active on-air callsign**.

It serves as a symbolic bridge between my roots and my new home:
* **ZP** is a tribute to my new home, Paraguay.
* **6** represents the radio region shared by both my home QTH and my new one here.
* **AHP** comes from my active callsign (**UA6AHP**), keeping my radio identity intact *(and fingers crossed, CONATEL will officially grant me this exact Paraguayan callsign in the future!)*.

### 💻 What You’ll Find Here & on Patreon / Boosty

While I’m launching a brand-new Spanish-language YouTube channel to connect with the local Latin American ham community, my GitHub, Patreon, and Boosty serve as our main hubs. Here and in our community, I share:
* 📐 **3D Models (STL)** — functional enclosures, antenna parts, and custom ham gear.
* ⚡ **Firmware & Digital Projects** — ready-to-flash builds and open-source hardware experiments.
* 📱 **Apps for Hams** — practical software and utilities built for everyday amateur radio tasks.
* 📖 **Setup Guides** — step-by-step documentation from my videos and lab tests.

Your support on **Patreon** or **Boosty** directly funds new radio gear, 3D printing materials, and the time it takes to test, code, film, and document open projects for all of us.
